A personal invitation

This isn't a tour. It's a homecoming.

Every spring, the mountains around Roseto start to bloom, and I open my village to a small group of new friends. We cook. We walk. We eat (a lot). We sit in the piazza like people used to, with time for one another.

I don't just put these trips on the internet and wait for people to show up. I get to know every guest first. That's why we start with a conversation.

Con amore, Dorina
Ten days at the table

Cook. Explore. Belong.

Village life in Southern Italy, with towns dating back more than a thousand years, and wine with every meal.

i.

Cook

  • Lessons with Mamma Dorina and Chef Giuseppe
  • Recipes from the village nonnas
  • Olive oil tasting: tell the good from the not so good
ii.

Explore

  • Walking tour of Roseto's history and traditions
  • Day trip to Lucera's Roman amphitheater
  • A long lunch at a local winery
iii.

Belong

  • Welcome dinner with the village
  • Market day in the streets
  • Evenings with the town's social clubs
